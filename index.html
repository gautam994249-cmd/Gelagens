Gelagen Ingredients

1

Biotin:

Also referred to as vitamin B7, biotin is a water-soluble member of the vitamin B family and plays a role in metabolism of fats, carbohydrates, and protein in the body. It is found in many beauty supplements since it helps maintain normal physiological activities linked to healthy skin and hair. As per Gelagen, each two-gummy serving of the supplement contains 5,000 micrograms of biotin, which is a very large amount of the substance relative to the recommended intake. Deficiency of biotin can cause hair loss and skin issues; however, deficiency is rare, and no research has established whether extra biotin helps with hair and nail growth. Large amounts of biotin also affect several lab tests such as thyroid and cardiac tests.
2

Vitamin A:

Vitamin A is one of the vitamins that help support healthy vision, immunity, and cell development. Besides, this vitamin helps in the maintenance of normal skin and other body tissues. According to Gelagen, vitamin A is in the form of retinyl acetate at 630 micrograms for each two gummies as described in the label. Enough vitamin A is necessary; however, taking more than required is harmful since preformed vitamin A tends to get stored in the body. Pregnant women, women planning to conceive, or individuals on other vitamin A supplements require consultation to avoid taking combined doses of vitamin A. It is also noteworthy that vitamin A does not provide an assurance for anti-aging and that it needs to be considered with other nutrients obtained from diet and other sources.
3

Vitamin C:

Vitamin C is a water-soluble vitamin that supports normal immune function and acts as an antioxidant. One of its important roles is helping the body produce collagen, a structural protein found in skin, connective tissue, and other parts of the body. Gelagen's published label lists 20 milligrams of vitamin C per two-gummy serving. This makes vitamin C relevant to the product's general skin-health positioning, although it does not guarantee visible improvements in skin texture or firmness. Vitamin C is also found in foods such as citrus fruits, strawberries, bell peppers, and broccoli. A varied diet remains an important source of this nutrient. The benefit of additional supplementation depends on total intake and individual nutritional needs.
4

Vitamin D:

Vitamin D aids the body in calcium absorption, bone, muscle, and immune system functions. In addition, it plays a role in biological functions associated with cell growth and differentiation. According to Gelagen, vitamin D is also known as cholecalciferol, or vitamin D3, and is listed as 10 micrograms in every two-gummy serving. Even though vitamin D is crucial for optimal health, it would be inappropriate to market vitamin D as a surefire solution to improving skin and hair quality. The need for vitamin D is individual, and it depends on many aspects like diet, sun exposure, age, and personal health conditions. The test and supplementation could prove useful for those individuals who believe that they are deficient in this vitamin.
5

Vitamin E:

Vitamin E is an antioxidant nutrient that is fat soluble and protects cell membranes from oxidation. It occurs naturally in foods such as nuts, seeds, and vegetables. Gelagen contains 7.4 milligrams of vitamin E per two gummies. The antioxidant property is important for the maintenance of healthy cells, but it does not guarantee that taking the supplements will get rid of wrinkles and sun-damaged skin. Skin care also entails restricting exposure to too much ultraviolet rays, wearing sunscreen, and eating a nutritious diet. Those who are on anticoagulant drugs are advised to consult with a medical expert prior to consuming any supplement, especially those containing vitamin E.
6

Vitamin B6:

The nutrient vitamin B6, known as pyridoxine, plays an essential role in protein metabolism and formation of neurotransmitters, as well as other physiological functions. Vitamin B6 acts on the body for processing amino acids and normal metabolic function. According to Gelagen, each two-gummy contains 2 milligrams of vitamin B6. Although the nutrient vitamin B6 is included in some beauty formulas, its inclusion does not mean that the supplement will make the hair thicker, skin clearer, and fingernails harder. Vitamin B6 can be found in various food products, like poultry, fish, potatoes, bananas, and chickpeas. In evaluating the supplements, it is necessary to add up the total quantity of the nutrient. Overdose of vitamin B6 leads to nervous disorders.
7

Vitamin B12:

B12 is necessary for healthy red blood cells production, proper neurological system functioning, and DNA creation. According to Gelagen's list of ingredients, its B12 type is methylcobalamin, and the quantity of B12 contained in each serving (two gums) is 6 micrograms. It is especially important for people who have high risks of deficiency due to their dietary habits or certain medical problems. Nonetheless, vitamin B12 is not proved to be effective for cosmetic reasons of hair loss or skin aging in people who are not deficient in it. Natural sources of the vitamin are fish, meat, eggs, milk, and fortified products. Vegans need extra care with regard to B12 consumption. People who suffer from constant tiredness or numbness should seek professional help.
